About Gordon Howe

Gordon Howe prepares illustrated and annotated editions of classic literature, bringing historical works to contemporary readers with added visual context, editorial notes, and accessible presentation. The Forgotten Classics collection celebrates enduring works of fiction, philosophy, self-improvement, and speculative literature.

Original authors retain credit for their works. Gordon Howe's contribution is identified on each edition as illustrator and annotator.
